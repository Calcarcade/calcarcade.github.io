02About two minutes

Install

  1. Download

    Grab one game or all five. Each game is a single .nwa file. If you took the all-in-one zip, unzip it first.

  2. Open the app page

    In Chrome or Edge on a computer, go to my.numworks.com/apps

  3. Plug in and send

    Connect the calculator with its USB cable, add the .nwa files, and send them to the calculator.

  4. Play

    The games show up on the calculator's home screen. Press Home on the calculator to leave a game.

What you need

The calculator, its USB cable and a computer with Chrome or Edge. Your normal calculator apps and files are not touched, and all five games use about half of the 2.5 MB app space. If the calculator is ever reset, the games are removed, so just send them again.
